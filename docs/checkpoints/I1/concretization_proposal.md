# Concretization Proposal

MovingTrees is a Light-Mobility Fleet-as-a-Service platform focused on supporting the maintenance and preservation of urban green spaces, including public parks, gardens and recreational areas. The project aims to combine sustainable urban mobility with more efficient management of municipal green spaces by providing a fleet of electric vehicles adapted to the needs of outdoor maintenance and inspection activities.

The proposed scenario involves a fictional municipality that contracts MovingTrees to provide and manage a fleet of electric bicycles, cargo bikes and utility tricycles. These vehicles allow municipal workers to travel between different green areas, transport gardening equipment and perform maintenance and inspection tasks without relying on conventional motor vehicles. The fleet is distributed across designated hubs located near parks and other green spaces, making vehicles accessible whenever and wherever they are needed.

Through the platform, users can find available vehicles, check their characteristics and battery levels, make reservations and manage their trips. The system also provides fleet management capabilities, allowing vehicle registration, availability monitoring, hub management and maintenance tracking. A telemetry simulator will generate vehicle data, including battery levels, GPS positions, lock status and fault events, enabling the platform to monitor vehicle conditions and automatically identify situations requiring attention. The platform will support maintenance management and provide the municipality with an overview of fleet utilization, availability and operational performance.
