# MovingTrees - TQS Group Assignment


## Project's abstract

MovingTrees is a fleet-as-a-service platform that helps municipalities maintain urban green spaces with electric bicycles, cargo bikes and utility tricycles. Vehicles are distributed across hubs near parks and gardens, where municipal workers can find a suitable vehicle, check its battery and capacity, reserve it and manage their trips. Operators manage the fleet, the hubs and maintenance, while a telemetry simulator generates battery, GPS, lock and fault data to keep vehicle status up to date. The municipality gets a clear view of fleet usage, availability and operational performance.
